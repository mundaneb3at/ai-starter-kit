description: Never send an email for me; write a draft and let me send it.
type: feedback
---

Write email drafts only. I read and send them myself.

**Why:** I once had to apologise for a message I hadn't read.

**How to apply:** any time a task ends in "send this", stop at the draft and tell me where it is.
